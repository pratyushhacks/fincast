Budget Model Drives Adoption The new MacBook Neo, priced at $599 ($499 for education), is likely fueling growth, AppleInsider reported . Unlike the MacBook Air and Pro, which target upgrades, the Neo is aimed at first-time users. Early Signs of Demand Industry feedback suggests strong interest, with some Intel Mac users upgrading and new customers entering the ecosystem. AppleInsider called it the “new king of budget laptops.” Analysts And Reviewers See Upside Deepwater’s Gene Munster said the lower-priced MacBook Neo could expand Apple’s presence in the education market, add about 0.5% to overall revenue, and contribute roughly $2 billion annually to the Mac segment without hurting margins . Wedbush analyst Dan Ives said Apple’s new AI-focused Mac lineup could drive the next upgrade cycle and expand its user base . He highlighted the new M5 chips as a key catalyst, noting significant gains in AI computing power and graphics performance, which position Macs for more demanding AI workloads. Ives also pointed to the lower-priced MacBook Neo as a way to attract new users, saying the broader lineup of AI-enabled devices could support growth and help push Apple’s stock toward its $350 price forecast.
