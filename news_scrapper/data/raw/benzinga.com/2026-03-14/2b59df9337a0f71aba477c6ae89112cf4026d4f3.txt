Gambling.com Group Limited (NASDAQ: GAMB ) reported better-than-expected earnings for the fourth quarter on Thursday . The company posted quarterly earnings of 30 cents per share which beat the analyst consensus estimate of 24 cents per share. The company reported quarterly sales of $46.236 million which beat the analyst consensus estimate of $46.057 million. Gambling.com said it sees FY2026 sales of $170.00 million to $180.00 million, versus market estimates of $185.309 million. Gambling.com shares fell 3.2% to close at $4.21 on Friday.
