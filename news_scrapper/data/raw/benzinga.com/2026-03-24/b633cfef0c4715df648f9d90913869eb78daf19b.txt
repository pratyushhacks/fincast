BofA Securities sees Apple Inc's (NASDAQ: AAPL ) upcoming product shifts and launch strategy as key drivers for future growth, despite a slight price target cut. Analyst Wamsi Mohan maintained Apple with a Buy and lowered the price forecast from $325 to $320. Foldable iPhone Could Drive Demand The analyst expects Apple to launch its first foldable iPhone in 2026, marking a major design shift that could drive strong early demand. He said the device will likely feature a 7.7–7.8-inch inner display, a slim profile under 10 mm when folded, and use Touch ID instead of Face ID. Mohan believes initial demand could reach 10 to 20 million units, significantly higher than competing foldables, supported by interest from China and premium iPhone users seeking larger screens. With his Buy rating, Mohan cites catalysts including AI at the edge, the foldable launch and capital returns. Launch Shift To Smooth Supply Chain He also expects Apple to change its launch cycle by introducing foldable and Pro models in September, while shifting base, Air, and "e" models to early 2027. He said this staggered approach should help smooth supply chain utilization and production cycles. Mohan adjusted his estimates to reflect the timing shift and higher pricing for the foldable, lowering his price forecast slightly.
