At a current reading of 14.6, the index remained in the “Extreme Fear” zone on Thursday, versus a prior reading of 17.3. The Fear & Greed Index is a measure of the current market sentiment. It is based on the premise that higher fear exerts pressure on stock prices, while higher greed has the opposite effect. The index is calculated based on seven equal-weighted indicators.
