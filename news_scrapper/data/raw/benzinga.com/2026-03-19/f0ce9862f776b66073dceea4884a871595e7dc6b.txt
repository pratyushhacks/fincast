Fold Holdings Inc (NASDAQ: FLD ) reported mixed results for the fourth quarter on Tuesday . The company posted quarterly losses of 8 cents per share which beat the analyst consensus estimate of losses of 14 cents per share. The company reported quarterly sales of $9.100 million which missed the analyst consensus estimate of $10.116 million. Fold Holdings shares fell 6.7% to trade at $1.04 on Wednesday. These analysts made changes to their price targets on Fold Holdings following earnings announcement. Cantor Fitzgerald analyst Brett Knoblauch maintained Fold Holdings with an Overweight rating and lowered the price target from $4.5 to $2.
