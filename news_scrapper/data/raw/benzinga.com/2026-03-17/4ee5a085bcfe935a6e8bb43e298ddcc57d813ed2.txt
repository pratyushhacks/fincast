The $34,000 Reality Of Modern Weddings O'Leary's advice comes as wedding costs remain high in the United States. According to The Knot's 2026 Real Weddings Study , which surveyed 10,474 couples, the average wedding costs about $34,200. That figure has held fairly steady — the average was about $33,000 in 2024. Guest lists play a major role in those costs. The study found the average wedding includes about 117 guests, which works out to roughly $292 per person once venues, catering and other expenses are factored in. That kind of spending, O'Leary argues, can quickly eat up money that could otherwise go toward building long-term financial stability. He reiterates that couples don't need a massive wedding and suggests sticking with a civil ceremony followed by a small celebration with close friends and family. The key, he explains, is keeping the guest list tight and focusing on people who actually matter to the couple. Invest First, Celebrate Bigger Later O'Leary's bigger point is about what happens to the money couples don't spend on the wedding. Instead of pouring it into a single day, he says that money should go toward long-term goals like saving for a home or investing. Couples looking to make smart financial choices before and after marriage can benefit from professional guidance. Platforms like Money Pickle connect users with fiduciary financial advisors who help families prioritize spending, plan for the future, and build a strong financial foundation together. In the video, he also pushes back on the idea that couples need to jump straight into expensive experiences after getting married. Those big, flashy vacations can wait, he suggests. A simple weekend trip is more than enough early on. Once a couple stabilizes their finances and gets their footing , that's the time to enjoy bigger purchases and trips. For O'Leary, the message is straightforward: start married life on solid financial ground. A civil ceremony, a "smart little party," and investing the difference may not look as glamorous as a huge wedding — but it can put couples in a far stronger position for the years ahead.
