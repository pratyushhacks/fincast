Delay is permission “The Centre’s crackdown on rising bank frauds signals urgency, but intent must translate into speed. Delays in sanction under the Prevention of Corruption Act and weak coordination with the Central Bureau of Investigation continue to blunt enforcement,” said Gauhar Mirza, a partner at law firm Saraf and Partners. “Meanwhile, mule account networks are outpacing the system. Unless approvals are fast-tracked and accountability fixed, fraudsters will stay a step ahead. In financial crime, delay isn’t procedural, it’s permission.”
