Apple investors want to see progress from Apple’s own AI strategy, said Charles Rinehart, chief investment officer of Johnson Asset Management, an Apple shareholder. He adds that, unlike rivals, Apple doesn’t have a cloud business to sell excess compute if it overspends on AI data centers. “If they can act as a toll road for providers of AI, then they’ll probably end up looking good long-term for not having the big capex overhang,” he said.
