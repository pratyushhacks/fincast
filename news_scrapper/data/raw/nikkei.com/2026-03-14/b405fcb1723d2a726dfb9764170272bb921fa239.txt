Companies Honda to pay suppliers as much as $10bn over scrapped EV models Changing market environment pushes Japanese automaker to revamp strategy Development on Honda's 0 Series Saloon and two other EVs has been stopped, necessitating payments to some parts suppliers that would have been involved in production. (Photo by Mayumi Tsumita) TOKYO -- Honda Motor expects to pay as much as 1.7 trillion yen ($10.6 billion) in compensation to suppliers as the automaker abandons development of three electric vehicles as part of an EV strategy reassessment that could cost the company 2.5 trillion yen in loss.
