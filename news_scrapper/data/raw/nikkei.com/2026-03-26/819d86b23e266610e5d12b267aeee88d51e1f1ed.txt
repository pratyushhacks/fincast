Electric vehicles Why Honda hit reverse on its full embrace of EVs Japanese automaker bet big on a future that turned upside down Honda President Toshihiro Mibe was reluctant to abandon his company's bold EV shift despite warning signs in the market. (Nikkei montage) TOKYO -- Honda Motor's abandonment of plans for a long-term shift away from gasoline engines toward electric vehicles reflects an unpredictable future that has left the automaker with expenses and losses of up to 2.5 trillion yen ($15.7 billion).
