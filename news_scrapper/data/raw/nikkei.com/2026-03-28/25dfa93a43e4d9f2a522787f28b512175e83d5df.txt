Semiconductors China chip sector targets 80% self-sufficiency with US in its sights Asian giant's ambitions include creating homegrown version of Dutch lithography giant ASML Naura Technology Group highlighted an expanded product lineup at Semicon China this week. (Photo by Shunsuke Tabeta) SHANGHAI -- China's semiconductor industry plans to achieve 80% domestic self-efficiency by 2030, according to a target set by 13 top company leaders, as the country seeks to catch up to the U.S. through technological development and capacity expansion.
