Rajkummar Rao and Keerthy Suresh
                                          | Photo Credit: AP and Special Arrangement Rajkummar Rao and Keerthy Suresh will be seen next in Raftaar , Amazon MGM Studios announced on Saturday, March 14, 2026. Directed by Aditya Nimbalkar, the film has been produced by Patralekhaa and will be released in theatres on July 24, 2026. The makers unveiled a logo of the film to make the announcement. As per a press release, the film is set in a “highly competitive business of education” and is “a gripping drama of ambition, love, and greed”. “At its heart lies a fast-rising start-up and a charged relationship between a driven man and an equally ambitious woman. As money, power, and greed spiral, their hunger to win begins to clash with love. The rise is intoxicating, but after the fall, the question is simple: can they fight their way back to the top, or is this where it all ends?”, read the synopsis shared by the makers. Written by Rohan Narula, Raftaar also stars Anurag Thakur, Rohan Verma, Tanya Maniktala, and Rajat Kapoor in pivotal roles. Published - March 14, 2026 03:14 pm IST
