Decimal Point Analytics, a provider of data analytics and technology solutions for the financial services industry, has launched Rakshak, its AI-powered investor protection platform designed to guard retail investors from ‘emotionally-driven and behavioural investment mistakes - including FOMO [fear of missing out]-driven trades, panic selling, and revenge trading’. “Rakshak leverages advanced artificial intelligence and machine learning to monitor investor behaviour in real time, predict and prevent harmful trading decisions before they occur, and autonomously rebalance portfolios toward each investor’s long-term goals,” the firm said. “Built to address India’s most pressing investor welfare challenge - 192 million retail investors participating in markets without any behavioural safety net - the platform democratises institutional-grade protection for portfolios as small as ₹10,000,” it added. “With the launch of Rakshak, we are redefining investor protection by putting the power of advanced AI directly in the hands of investors,” said Shailesh Dhuri, co-founder & CEO, Decimal Point Analytics. “Rakshak is not just a monitoring tool for institutions - it is a B2C-focused platform designed to empower individual investors with real-time market insights, predictive intelligence, and early risk alerts,” he said. “By leveraging AI-driven models, the platform can anticipate potential market movements, flag behavioural risk patterns, and caution users before losses materialise,” he added. Rakshak has the ability to automate decision-support through intelligent AI agents that continuously scan data, learn from market behaviour, and proactively guide users toward safer, more informed investment choices, the firm said.
