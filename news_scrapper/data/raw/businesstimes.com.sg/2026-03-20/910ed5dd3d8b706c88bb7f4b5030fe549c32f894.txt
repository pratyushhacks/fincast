Published Thu, Mar 19, 2026 · 09:50 PM [NEW YORK] Wall Street’s main indexes opened lower on Thursday (Mar 19) as crude prices soared on intensifying Middle East hostilities, reviving inflation worries that have prompted the Federal Reserve to take a more cautious stance on interest rate cuts. The rate-sensitive small-caps Russell 2000 index dropped 10 per cent from its all-time intraday high. The Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 90.3 points, or 0.20 per cent, at the open to 46,134.87. The S&P 500 fell 41.6 points, or 0.63 per cent, at the open to 6,583.12​, while the Nasdaq Composite dropped 281.4 points, or 1.27 per cent, to 21,871.037 at the opening bell.
