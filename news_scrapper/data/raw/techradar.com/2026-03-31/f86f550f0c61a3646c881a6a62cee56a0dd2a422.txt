Over the years I've owned iMacs and iPhones, iPods and iPads, MacBooks, and now a Mac mini — but even so, I wouldn't describe myself as an Apple fanboy. No, I'm just a lover of great technology, and when Apple gets it right, it gets it very right. Despite my long history with the brand, though, I've barely touched the surface of the many hundreds of devices it's released over a half century since it was formed on 1 April 1976, and I doubt I'm unusual in that regard.
