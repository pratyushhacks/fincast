In a world where technology evolves faster than traditional education systems can adapt, Tutorac is positioning itself as a transformative force in global IT training. With a mission to democratize access to high-quality technology education, the company is building what it calls the world’s first dedicated IT Training Marketplace—bridging learners, trainers, academia, and industry on a single, scalable platform. A Marketplace Model for the IT Era Founded by Vinay Ratnapu and Dr. Mallikarjun Goud Puppali , Tutorac was created to solve a persistent gap in the IT ecosystem: the disconnect between skilled professionals, aspiring learners, colleges, and corporations seeking specialized expertise. Unlike conventional edtech platforms that operate with pre-recorded, one-size-fits-all courses, Tutorac introduces a dynamic marketplace model. Here, IT trainers (referred to as “Tutors”) can list their expertise across emerging and in-demand technologies, while learners can directly connect with industry professionals for live, interactive, and customized training experiences. This approach enables: • Direct engagement between subject matter experts and learners • Real-time, industry-aligned training • Flexible learning formats tailored to evolving tech trends • Global accessibility without geographical limitations Backed by Vision and Early Investment Tutorac’s ambitious roadmap is supported by $500,000 in seed funding, signaling early investor confidence in its marketplace-led approach to IT education. The funding has been instrumental in platform development, technology infrastructure, and global outreach. Vinay Ratnapu, known for his entrepreneurial vision in education technology, has consistently advocated for industry-integrated learning models that close the employability gap. Dr. Mallikarjun Goud Puppali brings deep academic insight and strategic leadership, reinforcing Tutorac’s commitment to quality, accessibility, and global scalability. Together, their complementary expertise strengthens the company’s mission to decentralize IT education and make advanced technical skills universally accessible. Bridging Learners, Academia, and Industry In January 2026, Tutorac launched its integrated marketplace platform, positioning it as a “bridge” between four critical pillars: 1. Learners 2. IT Tutors (trainers and subject matter experts) 3. Colleges and academic institutions 4. Corporations and industry leaders This interconnected ecosystem allows colleges to collaborate with industry experts, companies to source specialized trainers, and learners to gain exposure to real-world use cases. By aligning academic instruction with industry demand, Tutorac addresses one of the most pressing challenges in IT education: employability. Tutorac was founded by Vinay Ratnapu and Dr. Mallikarjun Goud Puppali , who share a vision of transforming how IT skills are delivered and accessed globally. Combining entrepreneurial foresight with academic depth, the founders recognized the widening gap between industry requirements and traditional education models. Their mission with Tutorac is to democratize high-quality, industry-aligned IT training by building a marketplace that connects real-world experts directly with learners, institutions, and enterprises—making advanced technology education more accessible, practical, and globally scalable. Redefining Global IT Skill Development The IT landscape today demands constant upskilling in areas such as cloud computing, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, DevOps, data engineering, and emerging technologies. Traditional institutions often struggle to update curricula at the pace required by industry. Tutorac’s marketplace model, however, allows experts actively working in the field to share up-to-date knowledge directly with learners. This real-time relevance creates a significant competitive advantage for students and working professionals seeking career advancement. Empowering Trainers as Entrepreneurs Tutorac not only supports learners—it also empowers IT professionals. By providing a global platform where trainers can showcase their expertise, build their brand, and monetize their skills, the company is transforming subject matter experts into independent edupreneurs. This shift decentralizes IT education, reducing dependency on large institutions and enabling a more democratic knowledge economy. A Scalable Global Vision As digital transformation accelerates across industries worldwide, the demand for agile, industry-ready IT talent continues to grow. Tutorac’s scalable marketplace model is designed to serve global markets, breaking geographical barriers and creating cross-border learning opportunities. With strong founding leadership, early-stage funding, and a differentiated marketplace approach, Tutorac is redefining how IT training is delivered, consumed, and valued.
