EKA Mobility, a Pune-based electric vehicle and technology company, has been awarded the Letter of Award (LOA) for the supply and deployment of 915 electric buses in Hyderabad. The project will be executed under the Government of India’s CESL-led PM E‑DRIVE (Electric Drive Revolution in Innovative Vehicle Enhancement) scheme. The scheme is aimed at promoting electric vehicles, strengthening domestic manufacturing, and reducing urban pollution. EKA Mobility said it formed a consortium with GreenCell Mobility to take up the project. The company will deploy as many as 100 nine-metre and 815, 12 m standard floor non-AC electric buses, designed to cater to diverse urban transit requirements. Mitsui Co., Ltd. (Japan) & VDL Groep (Netherlands) are equity partners in EKA (Pinnacle Mobility Solutions Pvt. Ltd.). Zero‑emission buses “These fully electric, zero‑emission buses are engineered for efficient, reliable, and passenger‑friendly city operations,” the company said in a statement on Monday. The company, however, has not disclosed the value of the deal and the timeline of the bus deployment. This LOA follows earlier awards secured by EKA Mobility under CESL’s PM E‑Bus Seva initiative for the deployment of electric buses across Rajasthan, Andhra Pradesh, Odisha, Puducherry, and Meghalaya, according to Rohit Srivastava, Business Head & Chief Growth Officer, EKA Mobility.
