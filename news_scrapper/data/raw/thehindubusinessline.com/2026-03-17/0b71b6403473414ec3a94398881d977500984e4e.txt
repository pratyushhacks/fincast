Bengaluru-based IISc-incubated deep-tech startup Agnit Semiconductors raises $2.6 million in a seed extension equity round led by Shastra VC. Existing investors 3one4 Capital and Zephyr Peacock also participated in the round. AGNIT plans to use the funds to scale production of Gallium Nitride (GaN) components to 1 lakh units over the next 24 months. The company will also expand into telecom infrastructure and power semiconductor devices. AGNIT said it aims to move from pilot deployments to commercial production in the coming years. Commercial shipments of its first products are expected to begin in July 2027. The company was incubated at the Indian Institute of Science in Bengaluru. It has raised $7.47 million in total funding so far and now plans to shift its pilot products into volume manufacturing. Speaking on the fundraise, Hareesh Chandrasekar, CEO and Co-founder of AGNIT Semiconductors, said the company is entering a new phase. “This round comes at an important point in our journey as we move from technology validation to building scale,” he said. Chandrasekar said the firm’s GaN technology is based on more than 15 years of research at IISc. “What excites me most is seeing the science move into real systems, with three distinct radio-frequency products already in pilots across Indian defence platforms,” he said. The company plans to advance two GaN radio-frequency products from pilot to volume production over the next year. It will also start pilots of telecom-focused radio-frequency chips and begin field validation of a GaN power device. Published on  March 16, 2026
