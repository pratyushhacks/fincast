More importantly, the milestone marks the creation of a powerful global cybersecurity distribution platform that TAC Security believes will drive long-term enterprise value. In cybersecurity, building trusted access to customers at this scale is one of the hardest assets to create. TAC Security has built this client base through product strength, credibility, automation, and execution-led growth. The Company believes this installed base is not just a reflection of current scale, but a strategic platform that can be leveraged to introduce, cross-sell, and upsell future-ready security solutions across a large global ecosystem.
