Key features include multi-entity accounting, automated invoicing and reconciliation, budgeting and forecasting, compliance management, and real-time dashboards. Is cloud accounting software secure for enterprise use? Yes. Leading enterprise accounting suites employ advanced encryption, identity and access controls, and undergo regular security audits.
