Why wider beats taller this time Samsung appears to be betting that a wider shape beats a taller screen. The 7.6-inch main display comes in slightly under the Z Fold 7’s expected 8-inch panel, but the proportions are what really stand out here. Flagship power inside that wider frame Under the hood, the Wide Fold isn’t cutting corners to achieve that wider design.
