SpaceX has in recent years become an aerospace powerhouse, securing billions of dollars in US government contracts and space projects. The firm has other companies in its stable like xAI, which owns social media platform X and artificial intelligence (AI) firm Grok. SpaceX also owns satellite firm Starlink, which provides internet services around the world. The SpaceX share sale could make Musk the world's first trillionaire. He currently has a fortune of more than $820bn, according to Forbes, which tracks the wealth of the world's richest people. In 2025, Musk became the first person ever to achieve a net worth of more than $500bn.
