See at Amazon Pure Comfort A middling pair of over-ear headphones can get really uncomfortable really quickly, from sheer weight, poor fit, overheating, or a combination of all three. When the AirPods Max first arrived on the scene five years ago, the reviews raved about their exquisite comfort almost as much as their pro-level sound. The aluminum ear cups, knit-mesh headband, and memory foam cushions are all built for lengthy yet enjoyable use. The cushions create a seal that makes a difference acoustically, and the headband distributes weight well enough that extended listening sessions don’t turn into endurance tests. Of course, they’d be little more than glorified earmuffs if they didn’t come with truly superior sound. The Active Noise Cancellation is top-notch thanks to the H1 chip, and Personalized Spatial Audio with dynamic head tracking means the soundstage stays anchored to the content you’re watching rather than rotating with your head. This latest USB-C version of the AirPods Max also adds Lossless Audio with ultra-low latency over a wired connection, which the original Lightning model lacked. One Big Happy Family The AirPods Max are seriously good wireless headphones even when you use them with non-Apple devices, but it’s how they work within that ecosystem that makes them a true must-have for people with the iPhone/Mac/iPad trifecta. You pick them up, they pair automatically by proximity, and switching between your iPhone, iPad, and Mac happens without any manual intervention. Audio pauses when you take them off.
