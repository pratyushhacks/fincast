Posted Mar 18, 2026 at 4:58 PM UTC SwiftKey will soon require a Microsoft account to save your typing info. Starting May 31st, 2026, the Microsoft-owned SwiftKey will stop supporting Google and Apple accounts, according to Windows Central . Though you don’t need an account to use SwiftKey on its own, you’ll need to sign in with Microsoft to continue syncing your most-used words across devices and receive personalized typing suggestions.
