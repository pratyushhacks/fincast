Posted Mar 16, 2026 at 7:11 PM UTC Apple has acquired MotionVFX, a third-party Final Cut Pro plugin developer. On Monday, MotionVFX announced on its website that it’s “joining the Apple team.” MotionVFX is the developer behind a suite of tools for Apple-owned Final Cut Pro, including several AI features like AI upscaling and captions. The suite is currently still available as a monthly subscription catered toward creators, similar to Apple’s Creator Studio subscription.
