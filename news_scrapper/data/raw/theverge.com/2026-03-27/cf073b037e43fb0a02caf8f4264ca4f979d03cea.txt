Posted Mar 26, 2026 at 8:57 AM UTC Apple settles Vision Pro leaker lawsuit. The Cupertino company sued former Vision Pro engineer Di Liu last year for allegedly stealing trade secrets before starting a new role at Snap. The case was dismissed this week after Liu agreed to return Apple’s confidential information and pay the company an undisclosed sum for monetary damages.
