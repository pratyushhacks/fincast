Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has laid out new plans for the future of the world's most valuable company at its annual GTC conference in Silicon Valley. They include restarting the manufacturing of an AI chip for the Chinese market and continuing to develop the technology used in autonomous vehicles. Ira Spitzer reports from San Jose, California. Brad Gastwirth - Global Head of Research & Market Intelligence, at Circular Technology also joined us from Delray Beach, Florida.
