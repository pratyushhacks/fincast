GATE 2026 Results: Financial Assistance for Postgraduate Studies A valid GATE score also makes candidates eligible for financial assistance during postgraduate studies. MTech students receive a stipend of Rs 12,400 per month, typically for a duration of 22 months For direct PhD programmes, candidates receive Rs 37,000 per month for the first two years The amount increases to Rs 42,000 per month from the third to the fifth year These benefits are provided for programmes in engineering, technology, science, humanities and related disciplines at institutions supported by the Ministry of Education and other government bodies.
