With Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 : The Culling Game Part 1 heading toward its finale, the anime has introduced new characters and voice cast members through its latest trailer, setting the stage for an expanded final episode later this month. The newly announced voice cast includes Hiroki Tōchi as Ryū Ishigori, Nana Mizuki as Takako Uro, and Yukihiro Nozuyama as Kurourushi. VIDEO The final episode of the current arc will air as an extended 27-minute special on March 26, marking the conclusion of the first part of Season 3. The anime premiered earlier this year with a one-hour special featuring its first two episodes on January 8. It aired on the “Super Animeism TURBO” programming block across MBS, TBS, and their affiliates, as per Anime News Network. The Jujutsu Kaisen franchise has maintained strong momentum since its debut. The first television anime season premiered in October 2020 and ran for 24 episodes. The prequel film Jujutsu Kaisen 0 debuted in Japan in December 2021, later releasing in North America in March 2022 with both subtitled and dubbed versions.
