The Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) will announce the Class 12 Topper List 2026 today, March 23, at 1:30 PM. The topper list will be released alongside the BSEB Intermediate Result 2026 during an official press conference led by the board chairman. Bihar Board Class 12th 2026 Stream-wise Topper List The board will publish separate topper lists for Science, Arts, and Commerce streams. These lists will include the names of students who have secured the highest marks in each stream, along with their basic details and scores. Science stream toppers Aditya Prakash Aman from Samastipur secured the top position in the BSEB Class 12 Science stream with 96.20%. Sakshi Kumari and Sapna Kumari jointly held the second position, each scoring 479 marks. Arts stream toppers Nishu Kumari from Gaya has secured the top position in the BSEB Class 12 Arts stream this year, scoring 489 marks (95.8%). District-wise Details Also Available Along with the overall toppers, the board will also release district-wise topper lists, allowing students to check top performers from their respective regions. Top Performers in 2025 In the Bihar Board Class 12 Result 2025, students across all streams delivered outstanding results. In Science, Priya Jaiswal secured the top position with 484 marks (96.8%). In Commerce, Roshani Kumari topped with 475 marks (95%). In Arts, Ankita Kumari and Shakib Shah jointly held the first rank with 473 marks (94.6%). High Competition Among Students The previous year’s results highlighted intense competition and strong academic performance across streams, reflecting the consistent excellence of Bihar Board students.
