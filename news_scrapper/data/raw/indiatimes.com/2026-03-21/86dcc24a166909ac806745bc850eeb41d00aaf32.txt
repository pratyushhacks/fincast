Kolkata: The ECI on Friday constituted 19 Appellate Tribunals across 24 districts of West Bengal to hear appeals against decisions taken by judicial officers during the judicial adjudication under the Special Intensive Revision exercise of electoral rolls. Meanwhile, the first supplementary list is likely to be published on Monday, followed by the second list next on Friday. According to ECI sources, supplementary lists for around 40,000 booths have been prepared. A total of 27.23 lakh cases have been adjudicated by judicial officers appointed by the Calcutta High Court.
