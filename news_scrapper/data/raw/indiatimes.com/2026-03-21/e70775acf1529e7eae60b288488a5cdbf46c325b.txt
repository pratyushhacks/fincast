Bengaluru: Kerala chief minister Pinarayi Vijayan has announced assets worth Rs 1 crore in his affidavit before the election commission. This includes both his movable and immovable assets. Vijayan is seeking re-election from Dharmadom in Kannur district , where he filed his nomination papers as LDF candidate on Thursday. His wife Kamala Thayikandiyil has declared assets of about Rs 90 lakh. He is also pursuing an appeal in a CBI case in the apex court. The CM’s earnings have risen by about 85% between 2020-21 and 2024-25. The CM has also declared a cash balance of Rs 10,000 in his possession.
