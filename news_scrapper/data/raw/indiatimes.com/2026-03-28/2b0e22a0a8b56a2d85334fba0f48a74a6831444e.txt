NEW DELHI: Dutch chipmaking tools company ASML is actively considering partnering with Indian companies, said Union Minister of Electronics & IT Ashwini Vaishnaw . “Had detailed discussions on how ASML can support India Semiconductor Mission . Briefed them on India's semiconductor journey so far and future plans,” Vaishnaw said on microblogging platform X on Thursday. “ASML is actively looking to partner with Indian companies. Their team will be visiting various semiconductor units and companies across India,” he added. ASML builds the most advanced lithography machines , which are critical to transforming silicon wafers into semiconductor chips . The Prime Minister Narendra Modi-led government, as part of the India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) 2.0, is focusing on developing a robust homegrown design ecosystem, materials, equipment, chemicals, and gases required for the industry. The second edition of the ambitious programme is already in the preparatory phase. The government has provisioned ₹1,000 crore for ISM 2.0, as part of the Union Budget for FY26-27. In January this year, a delegation led by Vaishnaw visited ASML’s headquarters in Veldhoven to gain a better understanding of its technology, as its machines would be deployed at the Tata Electronics-PSMC chip fabrication plant in Dholera.
