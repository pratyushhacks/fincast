Eicher Motors’s motorcycle arm Royal Enfield on Friday launched the 2026 Guerrilla 450 , introducing a new APEX variant alongside updates to its existing lineup, with bookings opening immediately and retail sales set to begin from March 31. The headline addition is the Guerrilla 450 APEX, positioned as a sportier, more road-focused version of the street-performance roadster. It comes with a revised riding posture, road-biased tyres, and updated ride modes aimed at improving handling and agility in urban conditions. The APEX variant features new 17-inch Vredestein Centuro ST tyres, a more forward-leaning riding stance with lowered handlebars, and refreshed styling elements including new colour options — APEX Red, Black and Green. The company has also introduced ride mode retention, allowing riders to seamlessly switch between Street and Sport modes. Existing Dash and Flash variants have also been upgraded with improved CEAT tyres designed for better grip, particularly in wet conditions. The Dash variant additionally gets a new “Twilight Blue” colourway. Across the range, the Guerrilla 450 continues to be powered by the Sherpa platform engine, delivering 40PS and 40Nm, and is built on a steel twin spar frame aimed at balancing stability and agility. All variants come equipped with a 4-inch TFT “Tripper Dash” display with Google Maps-powered navigation and smartphone connectivity. Check price (ex-showroom Chennai): APEX: Rs 2.49 lakh to Rs 2.56 lakh Dash: Rs 2.49 lakh to Rs 2.67 lakh Flash: Rs 2.72 lakh Royal Enfield said the updated lineup is aimed at sharpening the motorcycle’s street-focused appeal while offering riders more choice between upright ergonomics and a sportier, corner-focused setup.
