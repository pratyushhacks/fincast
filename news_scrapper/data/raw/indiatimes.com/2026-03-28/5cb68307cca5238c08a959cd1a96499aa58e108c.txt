While large chip manufacturers such as ⁠Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co and Intel focus on making the fastest and smallest computing chips, GlobalFoundries and Tower Semiconductor are specialty chipmakers that focus on niches such as radio-frequency chips and silicon photonics.
