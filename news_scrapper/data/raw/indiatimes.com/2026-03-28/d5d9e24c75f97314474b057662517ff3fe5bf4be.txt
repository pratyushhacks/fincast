New Delhi: Realty firm Arvind SmartSpaces Ltd will redevelop a housing society in Mumbai and it expects a revenue of around Rs 300 crore from free saleable area . In a regulatory filing on Friday, the Ahmedabad-based company informed that it has signed a society redevelopment project in Santacruz, Mumbai. Arvind SmartSpaces has real estate developments across Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar, Baroda, Bengaluru, Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR) and Pune.
