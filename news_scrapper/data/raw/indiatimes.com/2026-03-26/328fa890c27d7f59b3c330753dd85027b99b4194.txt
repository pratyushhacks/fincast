CJ Darcl Logistics on Tuesday inducted its first heavy-duty battery electric vehicle (BEV) in partnership with Volvo Trucks , as part of its push towards sustainable logistics operations. The move marks CJ Darcl’s transition from pilot projects to the deployment of higher-capacity electric vehicles within its logistics network. The newly inducted truck is the first heavy-duty BEV in the company’s fleet and also the third such vehicle introduced by Volvo Trucks in India. “With this induction, we are moving towards integrating cleaner technologies into mainstream operations. We are on a mission to become a carbon-neutral company and are actively working towards achieving this goal while setting an example for the logistics industry," said Nikhil Agarwal, President, CJ Darcl Logistics . The development builds on CJ Darcl’s earlier initiatives in electric mobility. In 2024, the company had deployed electric vehicles for intra-city aftermarket supply operations in Bengaluru, demonstrating the feasibility of EVs in its logistics network. The heavy-duty electric truck deployment follows an 18-month collaboration between CJ Darcl and Volvo Trucks, involving technical evaluations, route assessments, payload optimisation, and system integration to ensure operational readiness. The company said the induction aligns with its broader decarbonisation strategy, which focuses on reducing Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions. Integrating electric vehicles into its multimodal logistics network is expected to support low-emission freight movement while aligning with customers’ sustainability goals and India’s carbon reduction targets.
