Ola Electric on Wednesday announced the expansion of its ‘Ola Insiders’ upgrade programme to over 150 cities across India following strong initial demand, with more than 50,000 customers showing interest within days of its launch. According to the official press release, the move is expected to cover about 80 per cent of the company’s existing customer base, allowing users of earlier scooter models to upgrade to newer electric two-wheelers through its retail network. The ‘Ola Insiders’ programme includes incentives for existing customers, allowing those with older-generation scooters to upgrade to newer models with benefits of up to ₹50,000. Additional benefits of up to ₹20,000 are available for customers purchasing another vehicle under the same registration. It added that customers can also avail referral benefits and earn up to ₹5,000 in Ola credits upon successful delivery. The referred buyer will also receive a ₹1,000 cashback. The programme is aimed at existing customers of older-generation vehicles, enabling them to switch to the latest models while also offering additional incentives for repeat purchases and referrals. “We are excited to take this programme to over 150 cities, making it easier for 80 per cent of our customers to upgrade to our latest products and continue their electric journey with Ola,” said an Ola Electric spokesperson. With the expansion, customers can access the upgrade programme through Ola Electric’s retail network, enabling evaluation and vehicle upgrades across multiple cities. Ola Electric currently offers a portfolio of Gen 3 S1 scooters and Roadster X motorcycles . The Gen S1 3 scooter lineup is priced from about ₹85,000 to ₹1.78 lakh, covering both mass-market and premium variants. The Roadster X motorcycle range is priced between roughly ₹79,999 and ₹1.90 lakh, depending on the battery configuration.
