NEW DELHI: Nokia has elevated Samar Mittal as country business leader for India and appointed Vibha Mehra as country manager for India, as part of a new leadership model in the country, as the Finnish telecom gear maker looks to deepen its foothold in artificial intelligence (AI) and cloud segments. The appointments will be effective from April 1, 2026, Nokia said. Mittal will be responsible for executing Nokia’s go-to-market strategy in India and shaping its strategic direction with telecom operators, AI and cloud providers, and mission-critical enterprises. He will focus on deepening relationships with Nokia’s strategic partners in India, capturing new growth opportunities, and aligning Nokia’s portfolio and execution with the evolving needs of the Indian market. Before this appointment, he served as vice president at Nokia, where he most recently led the cloud and network services business across the Middle East and Africa region. Mittal has over 30 years of experience. In turn, Mehra will drive the vendor’s communications strategy, spearhead government relations, and strengthen external and public relations. She will also shape the India site strategy, advance the people agenda, and steer corporate social responsibility (CSR) priorities. Before this appointment, Mehra served as vice president for government relations at Nokia, where she led the company’s geopolitics and government relations function across the Asia Pacific region. She has over 26 years of experience across multinationals such as Microsoft, Intel, and Tata Consultancy Services (TCS). “Together, the two leaders will steer Nokia’s presence and impact in India,” Nokia said, adding that the top executives will work closely to drive the company’s strategic priorities.
