Mumbai: HDFC Bank will hold multiple board meetings over the next month to review decisions taken in recent years and not hide anything after chairman Atanu Chakraborty ’s resignation last week raised corporate governance issues, managing director and CEO Sashidhar Jagdishan said.. “We will take stock of past matters and address all issues—existing or new—without hiding anything,’’ Jagdishan said in an interview with ET. “If these are operational issues, we will tighten design and controls.
