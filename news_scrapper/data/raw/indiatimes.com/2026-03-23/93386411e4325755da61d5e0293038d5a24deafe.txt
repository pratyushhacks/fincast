The Indian National Congress on Saturday released its fifth list of candidates for the upcoming Assam Assembly elections 2026 , naming seven nominees across constituencies including Gossaigaon, Bijni and Katigorah. According to the list issued by the All India Congress Committee, candidates include Joseph Hasda (Gossaigaon), Birkhang Boro (Dotma-ST), Rajat Kanti Saha (Bijni), Anchula Gwara Daimary (Bhergaon), Narayan Adhikari (Mazbat), Nirmal Langthasa (Haflong-ST) and Amar Chand Jain (Katigorah). The latest list comes as the party steps up preparations in the final stretch of campaigning, amid heightened political activity and shifting alliances in the poll-bound state. Polling for the 126-member Assam assembly will be held on April 9, with counting scheduled for May 4, 2026. The ruling BJP-led alliance, headed by Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma, is seeking to retain power after its victory in 2021. He is contesting from the Jalukbari constituency. Gogoi is contesting from the Jorhat seat. With multiple candidate lists already out, parties are now expected to intensify ground campaigns, with the focus shifting to constituency-level battles in what is shaping up to be a closely watched contest.
