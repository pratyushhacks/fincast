New Delhi: Russia on Sunday targetted Ukraine following six of its citizens being arrested in India on charges of terrorism and alleged that Kiev is destabilising the region. Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova said that the "neo-Nazi" government of Ukraine wants to keep the "questionable activities of its citizens under wraps." "They were reportedly involved in supplying EU-made drones, training in drone assembly/repair, and conducting electronic warfare," said Zakharova.
