Battery swapping solutions provider SUN Mobility on Monday said that it has received industry’s first AIS 038 certification from the Automotive Research Association of India (ARAI) for an indigenously developed and designed swappable high-voltage battery platform, for truck and bus applications, in a development seen as a boost to the country’s commercial electric vehicle (EV) sector. The indigenously developed platform is designed for heavy commercial vehicles , including buses and trucks, and uses a battery-swapping model aimed at reducing downtime and improving operational efficiency. It is available in 50 kWh and 100 kWh configurations and operates in the 660V high-voltage category, with compatibility for both new vehicles and retrofitted fleets. “Induction of swap-enabled Heavy Electric Vehicles will be imperative to bring EV adoption from the margins to mainstream,” said Ashok Agarwal, CEO, SUN Mobility - HEV Business. The certification comes amid growing policy support for electrification of commercial transport, including government initiatives such as the PM e-DRIVE scheme, which seeks to accelerate adoption of electric buses and trucks. “Achieving AIS 038 certification for an indigenously designed system validates the robustness of our engineering and our ability to build scalable, modular solutions tailored for real-world operating conditions,” said Naveen Chopra, Chief Technology Officer & Chief Operating Officer (Manufacturing Operations), SUN Mobility - HEV Business.
